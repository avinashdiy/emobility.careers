## What is the Hybrid Recruitathon?

A multi-day, multi-city EV-industry hiring event run on emobility.careers. Two ways to participate, both delivering the same pipeline:

- **Offline (in-person)** — show up at the venue in Pune, walk the booths, do interviews at the company tables
- **Online (virtual)** — register and run the whole loop from anywhere (Delhi, Bengaluru, your hometown): browse the same booths, book interview slots that have Meet links, do the interview from your laptop

Both paths feed into the same recruiter pipeline. A Delhi student interviewing online for a Tata EV role and a Pune student doing the same role at the venue are in the SAME shortlist on the recruiter's screen. Hires count equally.

### Three roles, three verbs

| Role | What you do | Main pages you'll use |
|---|---|---|
| **Student** | *Attending* — find roles, apply, interview, get hired | `/fairs/[slug]`, `/fairs/[slug]/register`, `/me/fairs/[slug]/pass` |
| **Employer** | *Conducting* — staff a booth, post roles, run interviews, hire | `/employer/fairs/[id]`, `/employer/fairs/[id]/slots`, `/employer/applications/[id]` |
| **TPO** | *Supervising* — onboard your cohort, track outcomes, chase laggards | `/tpo`, `/tpo/programs`, the TPO referral link |

# For students — attending the Recruitathon

You can attend the Pune Recruitathon **at the venue (offline)** OR **online from anywhere**. The platform treats both equally — same booths, same recruiters, same hiring decisions.

## Before the event

### 1. Register for the fair

Two paths in:

- **Inline registration** at `/fairs/[slug]/register` — fastest. Creates your account + your fair registration in one form. ~2 minutes if your phone is handy.
- **Already on emobility.careers?** Open the fair page and click "Register" — one click.

**When you register, you'll pick your attendance mode:**

| Mode | When to pick it |
|---|---|
| 📍 **Offline (in-person at venue)** | You'll be in Pune on event day |
| 💻 **Online (virtual fair)** | You'll attend from home / a different city — every interview will be video or phone |
| 🌐 **Hybrid (either works)** | You can do either — picks up the widest slot pool |

> **Important**: your mode controls which interview slots you can see. If you pick OFFLINE and a Pune booth only has online slots, you won't see them. If you pick ONLINE, you can't book in-person slots. **HYBRID sees both**. You can re-register to change your mode if your plans shift.

You'll also be asked for:
- **Target roles** ("Battery Engineer, BMS Firmware, Vehicle Integration") — surfaces to recruiters so they can pre-target conversations
- **Willing locations** — cities you'd relocate to for the right offer
- **Year of study** + a couple of context questions

After registration you land at `/me/fairs/[slug]/pass` — **this is your fair pass**. Bookmark it; it's the page you'll come back to all event day.

### 2. Hit ≥60% profile completeness

Booth recruiters won't accept slot bookings from a thin profile (waste of their 30 minutes if your CV is missing). You need to clear the **fair eligibility gate**:

- [ ] Profile ≥60% complete
- [ ] CV uploaded (PDF or DOCX)
- [ ] Phone verified
- [ ] Email verified

The fair page has a clear "you're not eligible yet — finish these N items" panel with deep links. Most students hit 60% in 10–15 minutes from a half-filled profile.

### 3. Browse booths + apply to roles

Open the fair page at `/fairs/[slug]`. You'll see:

- **Featured companies grid** — every confirmed booth
- **Tracks** — filter chips for "Battery", "Embedded", "Sales & Service", etc.
- **Open roles** per booth — each role is a regular job listing
- **Speakers + partners + brochure downloads** — useful for talking points

To apply: click a role → Apply. Your CV + profile go through automatically. Some recruiters add **application questions** ("Tell us about a project where you debugged a battery thermal issue") — answers attach to your application.

You can apply to as many roles as you want. Each application starts in the **APPLIED** stage and moves through the recruiter's pipeline (Screened → Shortlisted → Assessment → Interview → Offer → Hired).

### 4. Book interview slots

From each booth card, click **"See interview slots"**. You'll get the booth's slot picker.

**What you see depends on your attendance mode:**

- **ONLINE candidate** sees only 📹 Video + 📞 Phone slots
- **OFFLINE candidate** sees only 📍 In-person slots
- **HYBRID candidate** sees everything with mode badges

If the booth has slots that don't match your mode, you'll see a hint: *"You registered as ONLINE, so only video/phone slots show here. To see everything, re-register from the fair page and choose HYBRID."*

**Slot booking tips:**
- Slots are 30 min by default. Some are pinned to a specific job, some are "talk to anyone at this booth".
- The most in-demand slots fill up days before the event. Book early.
- Conflict prevention: you can't book two slots at the same time across different booths. Cancel one before booking another.
- Booked slots appear on your fair pass + can be added to Google/Apple Calendar via the **"📅 Interview slots → calendar"** download.

### 5. (Online only) Test your camera + mic

If you're attending online, hit **🎥 Test camera + mic** from your fair pass. This opens `/me/fairs/[slug]/test-call`:

1. Click "Start test" — your browser asks for camera + mic permission
2. You should see your face + a moving mic-level bar when you talk
3. If anything's wrong, the page tells you exactly what (denied permission, no device, another app using the camera)
4. Click **"I'm ready"** → your fair pass gets a **✓ Setup tested** badge

**Why this matters**: recruiters running online booths see a green dot next to your name on their slot board once you've tested. They know you're not going to discover broken audio at 11:00 AM sharp.

Re-test as many times as you want. Recommended: test the night before, again 15 min before your first slot.

### 6. Watch for reminders

You'll get automated reminders by in-app + email at:
- **T-7 days** — "the fair is a week away, polish your profile"
- **T-3 days** — "pre-book slots before the in-demand booths fill"
- **T-1 day** — "tomorrow is the day"
- **T-1 hour** — "doors open soon"

If you're ONLINE, the day-before reminder also nudges you to test your camera + mic if you haven't yet.

## On event day

### If you're offline (at the Pune venue)

1. Open your fair pass on your phone: `/me/fairs/[slug]/pass`
2. **Show the QR code** at the check-in desk (or read out your 6-character code if the QR doesn't scan). You get a ✓ Checked in badge.
3. Walk to your booked-slot booth 5 minutes early. Bring photo ID + a printed CV.
4. After each interview, the recruiter marks you "Done" / "No-show" inside the platform — affects your pipeline status in real-time.

### If you're online

1. Open your fair pass: `/me/fairs/[slug]/pass`. The page **auto-checks you in** as soon as you open it during the event window — no action needed.
2. **Keep the tab open** — a tiny presence beacon tells the placement team and recruiters you're actively here. (Idle 5+ min = considered drifted away.)
3. For each booked online slot:
   - Wait until **30 min before the start time** — the "▶ Join interview" button lights up on the slot row
   - Click it — the recruiter's Meet/Zoom link opens in a new tab
   - For PHONE slots, the recruiter calls you on your registered phone. Keep it handy.

### If something breaks (online)

Online candidates have an SOS panel on the pass page: **"Something not working?"**

Use it when:
- Meet link won't load
- Video / mic suddenly stops working mid-interview
- Recruiter dropped off and isn't coming back

Click **"⚠ Report a connection issue"** → optional one-line description ("video frozen, they can't hear me") → Send. **The fair admin gets paged on WhatsApp + in-app within seconds** and will call you back on your registered phone, usually within a few minutes. They can also ask the recruiter to reschedule you to a later slot in the same day.

### Async fallback: video intro upload

If your slot got cancelled OR you couldn't book one in time, upload a **2-minute video intro** from the pass page. Recruiters at this fair see it as a non-blocking review item alongside your written application — "we couldn't meet you live but here's your face + voice".

Limits: under 2 min, under 50 MB, MP4 / MOV / WebM. Record on your phone's native camera, then upload from your phone or laptop.

## After the event

- Check your applications at `/me/applications` — pipeline updates land here as recruiters move you through stages. The page uses a kanban-style board (Applied · Screened · Shortlisted · Assessment · Interview · Offer · Hired) so you can scan all your stages at a glance; offers appear in their own column.
- Switch between Kanban and Timeline view via the toggle at the top of `/me/applications`
- If you got hired: 🎉 — your application stage flips to HIRED and the company gets a HIRED count on their analytics
- If you didn't hear back within 10 days, message the recruiter directly from their booth's company page

# For employers — conducting the Recruitathon

You're staffing a booth at the Pune Recruitathon. Your candidates come from two streams (offline Pune walk-ups + online from-anywhere bookings) and they merge into a single pipeline on your screen. The platform handles the routing; you just do the interviews + hire.

## Before the event

### 1. Accept the booth invitation

Admin invites your company → you get a notification at `/employer/fairs`. Open it, review the fair brief (date, venue, expected footfall, fair tracks). Click **Confirm** to lock your booth.

If your company isn't KYC-verified yet, **finish KYC first** — admin needs to approve before your booth goes public on the fair page. KYC is a one-time setup; future fairs reuse it.

### 2. Attach jobs to your booth

From `/employer/fairs/[id]`, attach the JobPostings you want to feature at this fair. You can:
- Attach existing OPEN jobs
- Create new fair-only jobs (mark them OPEN; they're discoverable on the public fair page)
- Pin jobs to **tracks** (Battery / Embedded / Sales) for the candidate-side filter chips

Each attached job gets a separate applicant pipeline. Applications from the fair come in tagged with `recruitmentDriveId = this-fair`, so post-event analytics segments cleanly.

### 3. Create interview slots

This is the most important pre-event step. Go to `/employer/fairs/[id]/slots`.

**Two ways to create slots:**

#### Bulk-generate (the 10 AM - 5 PM pattern)
The brochure-default workflow. One form:
- **Day** — first day of the fair
- **Start time (IST)** — e.g., 10:00
- **Each slot · minutes** — default 30
- **How many slots** — e.g., 14 (gives you 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM with a 30-min break around lunch)
- **Mode** — choose 📍 In-person at booth / 📹 Online — video / 📞 Online — phone
- **Meeting URL** (required for VIDEO) — paste your Meet/Zoom/Teams link
- **Pin to a job** (optional) — restrict slot to candidates applying to that specific role

Click **Generate slots** → 14 back-to-back AVAILABLE slots appear. Safe to re-run: existing slot times are skipped.

**For a hybrid booth**: run bulk-generate twice — once for in-person slots, once for video slots. Recruiter at the venue books the in-person queue; remote recruiter (or same recruiter from a laptop) handles the video queue.

#### Single slot — for one-offs
"Add an extra 4 PM Tuesday slot for that hot candidate I just met." Same fields, one slot.

### 4. Pre-screen applicants

Before the fair, candidates apply to your jobs. Each application surfaces:
- **Match score** — 0–100% (semantic match: candidate profile ↔ JD)
- **AI summary** — 4-sentence balanced verdict, auto-generated on first open (cached after)
- **"Why this candidate" pitch** — one-click pro-only pitch you can paste into Slack when championing a candidate to a hiring manager
- **Match explanation** — the JD bullets each candidate aligns to

Move candidates through stages: **APPLIED → SCREENED → SHORTLISTED → ASSESSMENT → INTERVIEW → OFFER → HIRED**. Each move auto-notifies the candidate in-app.

**Pro tip**: send shortlisted candidates a direct message before the event — "Looking forward to talking on Tuesday at 11. Anything specific you'd like to dig into?" Boosts attendance + grounds the conversation.

## On event day

### Open your slot board

URL: `/employer/fairs/[id]/slots`. This is your day-of command center.

At the top: **"⏱ Next 2 hours" rail**. Two columns — Online queue + In-person queue. Each row shows:

- The slot time
- 🟢 green dot — candidate has passed the camera/mic test (setup verified)
- 🔴 red dot — candidate hasn't tested yet (call out to them via in-app message)
- Pulse dot — candidate has the fair pass open right now
- Candidate name → clickable to their full profile
- **Join button** — opens the slot's Meet link in a new tab (online slots only)

This rail auto-refreshes when you reload the page (15-s refresh is honest enough for a fair-day pace).

### Running the interview

**For in-person slots**: candidate walks up to your booth. Use the slot board to confirm their name + booked time. After the interview, click **✓ Done** on the slot row.

**For online (video) slots**: 
1. ~5 min before the slot start, click **Join** on the slot row
2. The Meet link opens in a new tab — you're in the room first
3. At the slot time, the candidate clicks their own Join button and lands in the same Meet
4. Run the interview
5. After, click **✓ Done** on the slot row

**Three close-out buttons on every BOOKED slot:**

- **✓ Done** — interview happened; candidate stays in their current pipeline stage (move them later via the ATS)
- **↻ Reschedule** — expands an inline datetime picker. Pick a later time today (or tomorrow); the new slot is created, the candidate is notified. Use this when the candidate's connection failed, their previous interview ran long, or you simply ran out of time.
- **✗ No-show** — candidate didn't show / didn't join. Records absent for analytics. Reversible by admin if needed.

### Mid-event candidate-management

- **Move stages live**: From the slot row, click the candidate's name → application detail. Move them to INTERVIEW or OFFER directly while they're still on the call (or fresh in your memory after).
- **Take notes**: every application has a private team-notes thread. Drop quick observations ("strong on BMS, weaker on packaging") that your hiring manager will read.
- **Rate**: 1–5 stars per candidate. Drives the post-event shortlist.
- **Message**: open conversation with the candidate from inside the application detail. The chat thread is searchable later.

### Async review (post-slot)

If a candidate uploaded a 2-minute **video intro** (because their slot got cancelled, or they couldn't book one in time), their application detail page (`/employer/applications/[id]`) shows a **"🎥 Candidate uploaded a video intro · ▶ Watch intro"** card above the Resume panel. Click → the video streams inline (5-min presigned URL, refresh if expires). Watch like async homework when you have a free 10 min.

## After the event

- **Finalize pipelines**: every candidate you talked to needs an updated stage. INTERVIEW for "we want to bring them back", OFFER for "we want to hire", REJECTED for "not a fit". Outstanding applications stuck in APPLIED a week after the event are a bad signal — close them out.
- **Mark hires**: when you make an offer and the candidate accepts, flip them to **HIRED**. This is what counts in the post-event report and is the metric admin tracks on the live console.
- **Per-candidate notes**: end-of-event, write a 1-line summary on every interviewed candidate so a teammate can pick up the thread if you're out.

# For TPOs — supervising your cohort

You're a Training & Placement Officer at a college sending students to the Recruitathon. The platform gives you a referral link to share with students + a dashboard to track outcomes for your college.

## Before the event

### 1. Get your TPO account approved

Sign up at `/tpo` (or use the signup link in your invitation email). Submit:
- College name + city
- Your designation (Director / TPO / Placement Officer)
- Verification proof (institutional email, college website link)

An admin reviews + approves within 1 business day. Once approved, you'll see the TPO dashboard.

### 2. Get your referral link

Every approved TPO has a per-cell **invite token** generated automatically once your `CollegePlacementCell` is APPROVED. For each active fair, your `/tpo` dashboard shows a one-click-copy invite URL of the shape:

```
https://emobility.careers/fairs/[slug]/register?as=candidate&tpo=YOUR_INVITE_TOKEN
```

The actual link is rendered in the **Invite link card** on your `/tpo` dashboard — just hit Copy. Share via WhatsApp / email / your placement portal. Every student who registers using your link is **attributed to your college** (`viaTpoCellId = your-cell`) — they appear in your dashboard, your `/tpo/programs/[slug]` per-fair report, and the admin CSV export's "Via TPO college" / "Via TPO name" / "Via TPO email" columns.

### 3. Bulk-invite via CSV roster

Faster than asking students one-by-one. From the TPO dashboard:
- **Upload a CSV** of (email, optional name, optional course)
- The platform either finds the existing user account OR creates a shell account + sends them an invite email
- Each invited row lands as a `REGISTERED` registration with `source = "ROSTER_IMPORT"` attributed to your college

Tracking after upload: per-batch counters (rows parsed / invitations sent / successful registrations / failures with reason).

### 4. Drive completion of student profiles

Students need ≥60% profile completeness + CV + verified phone + verified email to book interview slots. **Most no-shows trace back to students who skipped this**.

Use the TPO dashboard's **funnel view**:
- Registered (came in via your link or roster)
- Profile complete
- Eligible for slot booking
- Booked at least one slot
- Checked in
- Interviewed
- Offered / Hired

Where the funnel narrows is where your follow-up calls land. If 200 registered but only 80 are eligible, you have 120 students to chase.

### 5. (Optional) Use the broadcast tool

Admin can send broadcasts targeted at TPO-referred candidates. Coordinate with the placement team if you want a "from-the-TPO" message blasted to your students ("Reminder: your interview is tomorrow, make sure your phone is charged").

## On event day

### Watch the dashboard

The TPO dashboard updates live — every 15 seconds in line with the admin live console. You see:

- Total students attending (registered + not-cancelled)
- Attendance mode breakdown (in-person / online / hybrid)
- Check-in rate (currently arrived)
- Online-active count (online students with the pass page open right now)
- Setup-tested count (online students who've verified camera/mic)
- Interview-slot bookings + completions

### Chase no-shows

A student who registered + didn't check in by 11 AM is likely to be a no-show by lunch. Call them. The dashboard surfaces their phone number directly so you can dial without leaving the page.

### Handle issues for your students

If a student's interview hits trouble and they file a connection-issue SOS, the platform admin handles the immediate response (calls them, asks the recruiter to reschedule). You'll see it in your funnel as "issue logged" — useful context but not blocking action.

## After the event

### Download the placement report

From the admin recruitathon report page (`/admin/recruitathon/[slug]/report`), you'll see a per-attendance-mode breakdown of every metric: registered → checked-in → interviewed → hired. The placement coordinator can hand you the **per-college slice** filtered to your students.

CSV exports include `viaTpoCellId` — that's the attribution key for your college. Outside admin support staff, this data ties back to:
- How many of YOUR students attended
- How many got interviews
- How many got offers / hires
- Where the funnel leaked (e.g., 100 registered, 95 completed profile, only 60 booked slots)

That last column is the one to take back to next year — it tells you where to fix your student-prep process.

# FAQ + troubleshooting

## Student FAQ

**Q: I picked OFFLINE but now I can't come to Pune. Can I switch to ONLINE?**
A: Yes. Re-register on the fair page (`/fairs/[slug]/register`) and pick a new mode. Your existing interview slots may not transfer if they were in-person — you'll need to re-book online slots. Do this ≥48h before the event so booths still have availability.

**Q: I picked ONLINE but I'm in Pune that day. Can I just walk in?**
A: Re-register as HYBRID. That unlocks both online + in-person slot pools, so you can book whichever's available.

**Q: I'm registered but I don't see any interview slots.**
A: Three possible causes:
1. You don't meet the eligibility gate — finish profile + CV + phone + email
2. Your attendance mode doesn't match what the booth offers — switch to HYBRID
3. The booth genuinely has no slots — message them from the fair page

**Q: My Meet link isn't working / the recruiter dropped off.**
A: Hit the **⚠ Report a connection issue** button on your fair pass. Optional one-line description. Within minutes, the fair admin will call you on your registered phone. They can also ask the recruiter to reschedule you to a later slot the same day.

**Q: I missed my slot. Am I done?**
A: Two recoveries:
1. The recruiter may mark you NO-SHOW or RESCHEDULE you to a later time. Watch your in-app notifications.
2. Upload a 2-min **video intro** from your fair pass. Recruiters see it as a non-blocking review item — your written application + the intro give them enough to make a call.

**Q: I'm at the venue but I can't get my QR code to scan.**
A: Read out the 6-character code under the QR. Fair staff types it into the scanner UI. If even that fails, give them your email — they can look you up.

**Q: My profile says 55% — what's missing for 60%?**
A: Open `/me/profile`. Each section shows its contribution to completeness. The fastest wins: upload a profile photo, add one more skill, write a 2-sentence headline.

## Employer FAQ

**Q: I created slots but they're not visible to candidates.**
A: Three checks:
1. Booth status must be CONFIRMED (not INVITED). Admin needs to confirm.
2. Slot status must be AVAILABLE (not CANCELLED).
3. Slot mode must match the registered candidate's mode (OFFLINE candidate won't see VIDEO slots).

**Q: Where do I paste the Meet link?**
A: At slot creation time. Both bulk-generate + single-slot forms have a "Meeting URL" field that's required when Mode = VIDEO. Use ONE link for all your slots that day (Meet/Zoom rooms support multiple consecutive uses), OR a per-slot link if you prefer fresh rooms.

**Q: A candidate's Meet froze mid-interview. What now?**
A: Click **↻ Reschedule** on their slot row → pick a later time today. The candidate gets notified automatically. Original slot flips to CANCELLED so you have a clean record.

**Q: How do I see who pre-tested their camera + mic?**
A: On `/employer/fairs/[id]/slots`, look at the dots next to each booked candidate's name:
- 🟢 green = setup tested
- 🔴 red = not tested yet (call them out via in-app message)
- Pulse = they have the fair pass open right now

**Q: A candidate uploaded a video intro. Where do I watch it?**
A: Open their application detail (`/employer/applications/[id]`). When the application came in through a fair AND the candidate uploaded an intro for that fair, you'll see a "🎥 Candidate uploaded a video intro — ▶ Watch intro" card above the Resume panel. Click ▶ Watch intro to load + stream the video inline. URL is presigned for 5 min — if it expires you can just click Watch again.

**Q: Can I bulk-message everyone who applied to my booth from one screen?**
A: Not directly — `/employer/fairs/[id]/candidates` is a read-only pre-screen view (filter by fair-mode availability, experience level, DIYguru-verified, EV-experience). To message a candidate, click their name → open their public profile → use the **Connect / Save / Message** buttons gated there, or open their application detail and start a thread from the "Message candidate" card. For a true mass send (e.g., "interview prep tips" to every registrant) coordinate with admin to fire a broadcast at the FAIR_REGISTERED_CANDIDATES audience — admin can mode-filter it (ONLINE only / OFFLINE only / HYBRID only).

## TPO FAQ

**Q: How do I know if a student registered via my link?**
A: The student's registration record carries `viaTpoCellId = your-cell-id`. In the TPO dashboard, the "Your students" view filters to only those. In the post-event report, you can download a CSV filtered to your college.

**Q: I uploaded a roster and some rows failed. Where do I see why?**
A: The roster import detail shows per-row failure reasons (e.g., "invalid email format", "student already registered for a different fair", "email already exists with a different attendance mode"). Re-upload the cleaned rows; existing successful rows are skipped (idempotent).

**Q: How do I download the per-college outcome report?**
A: Your `/tpo/programs/[slug]` page is the canonical per-college view — shows your students' funnel + outcomes for that fair. For a row-level spreadsheet, ask the fair organizer (DIYguru) to share the CSV from `/admin/recruitathon/[slug]/candidates.csv` — it includes the **Via TPO college / Via TPO name / Via TPO email** columns so you can filter to your students in Excel / Google Sheets in 10 seconds.

**Q: My students keep dropping at "profile complete" stage. What's missing?**
A: Most common: missing CV (they expect autofill from LinkedIn but the platform needs a PDF upload) and unverified phone (the SMS OTP flow has 1-2% deliverability issues in some carriers). Send them direct help links — `/me/profile` for both.

**Q: A student lost their phone during the event and can't access their pass.**
A: They can sign in from any device — the pass URL is `/me/fairs/[slug]/pass` on every device once signed in. If they don't have access to email for the magic-link sign-in, fair staff can look them up by name + check them in manually.

## All audiences — common issues

**"I'm not getting reminder emails."**
- Check spam / promotions tabs
- Confirm email is verified (un-verified accounts don't get emails)
- Check `/me/settings/notifications` — email channel must be on

**"The fair page isn't loading."**
- Hard refresh (Cmd+Shift+R / Ctrl+Shift+R)
- Try incognito to rule out a browser extension
- If still broken, the live console is the canonical "is the platform up?" check — if THAT page loads, the platform is fine

**"I can't log in."**
- Use the magic-link option (`/signin` → enter email → click link in inbox)
- If your email isn't recognised, your account was created via OAuth — try Google/LinkedIn sign-in
- If you've been on the platform via a roster import without ever signing in, use the **password reset** flow with your email; it lets you set a password and start fresh

# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Recruitathon** | A multi-day, multi-company EV-industry hiring event run on emobility.careers. Hybrid means both in-person + online tracks. |
| **Booth** | A `RecruitmentDriveCompany` record — one company's participation at one fair. Has its own slot pool + applicants. |
| **Slot** | A `RecruitmentDriveInterviewSlot` — a single 30-min interview window at a booth. Has a mode (ONSITE / VIDEO / PHONE) + optional meetingUrl + optional job-pin. |
| **Pass** | Your fair pass page at `/me/fairs/[slug]/pass`. Houses your check-in code + booked slots + Join buttons + SOS button. |
| **Check-in code** | 6-character alphanumeric (no I / O / 0 / 1 to avoid mis-reads). Encoded as a QR on your pass; also typed by hand at the desk. |
| **Attendance mode** | Your OFFLINE / ONLINE / HYBRID choice at registration. Controls which slots you can book + what reminder copy you get + how you check in. |
| **Setup tested** | You've completed the camera + mic self-test at `/me/fairs/[slug]/test-call`. Recruiter sees a green dot next to your name on their slot board. |
| **Eligibility gate** | The "60% profile + CV + phone + verified email" threshold required to book interview slots. Designed to keep recruiter time productive. |
| **TPO referral link** | A fair-scoped URL `?ref=YOUR_CODE` that attributes registering students to your college. |
| **Roster CSV import** | TPO bulk-invite flow. Upload a list of student emails; the platform creates accounts + invitations + fair registrations. |
| **Online check-in** | Automatic check-in for ONLINE / HYBRID candidates who open their fair pass during the event window. No scanner needed. |
| **Connection issue (SOS)** | Candidate-initiated alert on the fair pass when something breaks mid-event. Pages the fair admin on WhatsApp + in-app. |
| **Video intro** | Async fallback — a 2-min video the candidate uploads to substitute for a missed/cancelled interview slot. Recruiters watch it as homework. |
| **Reschedule** | Recruiter action that atomically cancels a booked slot + creates a new BOOKED slot at a different time for the same candidate. Notifies the candidate. |
| **Live console** | `/admin/recruitathon/[slug]/live` — auto-refreshing event-day dashboard for the placement ops team. KPIs, active-issues queue, broadcasts. |
| **Post-event report** | `/admin/recruitathon/[slug]/report` — per-attendance-mode breakdown of registered / checked-in / interviewed / hired. |
| **Application stage** | The pipeline status of a candidate's application: APPLIED → SCREENED → SHORTLISTED → ASSESSMENT → INTERVIEW → OFFER → HIRED (or REJECTED / WITHDRAWN). |
| **Broadcast** | Admin-initiated bulk message to a targeted audience. Can be mode-filtered ("send to ONLINE only") for fair-day announcements. |
| **AI summary** | Auto-generated 4-sentence balanced verdict on a candidate, surfaced on the recruiter's application detail. Cached after first open. |
| **Why this candidate** | One-click pitch the recruiter can paste into Slack/email when championing a candidate to a hiring manager. |
